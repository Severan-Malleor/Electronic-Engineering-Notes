# Capacitor

Q = C * V
C = Q / V
V = Q / C

### Capacitor Units
Q = Charge (Measured in Coulombs)
C = Capacitance (Measured in Farads)
V = Voltage (Measured in Volts)

# Watt's Law
P = V * I
V = P / I
I = P / V

### Watt's Law Units
P = Power (Measured in Watts)
V = Volts (Measured in Volts)
I = Current (Measured in Amps)

### Alternative (faster)
$P = V^2 / R$


---
# Ohm's Law
$V = I * R$
$I = V / R$
R = V / I

### Ohm's Law Units
V = Volts (Measured in Volts)
I = Current (Measured in Amps)
R = Resistance (Measured in Ohms $\Omega$)
