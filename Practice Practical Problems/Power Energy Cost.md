1. A 60 W LED bulb runs for 5 hours. 
- a) How much energy does it use? (in kWh) 
- b) At $0.12 per kWh, what is the cost?
Answer (a): $(60/1000) * 5 = 0.3 kWh$
Answer (b): $.3kWh * .12 * 100 = \textcent3.6$

2. A phone charger uses 10 W and is left plugged in for 24 hours. 
- a) How much energy is consumed?
- b) Cost at $0.10 per kWh?
Answer (a): $(10W / 1000) * 24hours = 0.24kWh$
Answer(b): $.24kWh * .10 * 100 = \textcent2.4$

3. A small fan is rated 72 W.
- a) How much current does it draw from a 12 V supply?
- b) If it runs for 8 hours, how many kWh does it use?
Answer (a): $72W = 12V * A$
* $72W/12V = A$
* 6 = A
* 6 Amps
Answer (b): $(72W / 1000) * 8 = 0.576 kWh$

4. You run a 1500 W space heater for 2 hours at a rate of $0.08/kWh. What is the total cost?
Answer: $(1500W / 1000) * 2 = 3kWh * .08 * 100 = \textcent24$
