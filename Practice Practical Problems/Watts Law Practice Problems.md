[[General Equations/Equations#Watt's Law|Watt's Law Equation]]

- A 9 V battery powers a circuit that draws **0.8 A**. How much power is being used?
Answer:
* $P = 9V * .8A$ 
* P = 7.2 Watts

- A device consumes **48 W** while running at **12 V**. What is the current?
Answer:
* $48W = 12V * A$
* $48W/12V = A$
* 4 = A
* 4 Amps

- An LED strip uses **0.25 A** at **5 V**. How much power does it consume?
Answer:
* $P = 5V * .25A$
* P = 1.25 Watts

- A resistor dissipates **1.2 W** with **0.3 A** flowing through it. What is the voltage across the resistor?
Answer:
* $1.2W = V * .3A$
* $1.2W/.3A = V$
* 4 = V
* 4 Volts
  
- A small fan is rated for **60 W** on a **12 V** supply. What current does it draw?
Answer:
* $60W = 12V * A$
* $60W/12V = A$
* 5 = A
* 5 Amps

- A phone charger outputs **10 W** at **5 V**. What current is supplied to the phone?
Answer:
* $10W = 5V * A$
  $10W/5V = A$
* 2 = A
* 2 Amps
