#### Basic Problems

A resistor has **12 V** across it and **0.2 A** flowing through it. How much power does it dissipate?

Answer:
* $P = 12V * 0.2A$
* $P = 2.4 W$

A 220 Ω resistor has **0.05 A** flowing through it. Calculate the power dissipated using $P=I^2R$

Answer:
* $P = .05^2 * 220 = .55 W$ or  550 mW

A 1 kΩ resistor has **9 V** across it. Calculate the power dissipated using $P = \frac{v^2}{R}$

Answer:
* $P = 9^2 / 1000Ω$
* $P = .081 W$ or 81 mW

An LED circuit has **3 V** across the resistor and draws **0.015 A**. What is the power dissipated in the resistor?

Answer:
* $P = 3V * .015$
* $P = .045 W$ or 45 mW

#### Intermediate / Practical Problems

A 470 Ω resistor has **5 V** across it.
a) Calculate power using $P = V^2 / R$
b) Calculate power using $P = I^2 * R$ (first find current)

Answer a:
* Watt's Law $P = 5^2 / 470$
* $P = .053W$ or 53 mW
Answer b:
* Ohms Law: $I = 5 / 470$
* $P = (.0106)^2 * 470 = .053W$ or 53 mW


You measure **0.03 A** flowing through a **330 Ω** resistor.
a) What is the power dissipated?
b) What voltage is across the resistor?

Answer a:
* Watt's Law: $P = .03^2 * 330 = .297 W$ or 297 mW
Answer b:
* Ohms Law: $V = .03 * 330 = 9.9V$

A small heater element has **12 V** across it and a resistance of **8 Ω**. How much power does it consume?

Answer:
* $P = 12^2 * 8 = 1152 W$ or 1.15 kW

An Arduino pin outputs **5 V** and drives a load that draws **0.018 A**. How much power is the Arduino pin supplying?

Answer:
* $P = 5 * .018 = .09W$ or 90 mW

A 2.2 kΩ resistor is dissipating **0.1 W**.
a) What current is flowing through it? 
b)What voltage is across it?

Answer a:
Reference: [[Math Notes#Isolating Squared Variables (Solving Equations with Exponents)|Isolating Squared Variables]]
* $0.1 = I^2 * 2200$
* $\sqrt{0.1 / 2200}$ = I
* .0067 = I
  .0067 A or 6.7 mA

A servo motor is stalled and draws **0.8 A** from a **6 V** supply. How much power is it consuming (and turning into heat)?

Answer:
$P = .8 * 6 = 4.8 W$
