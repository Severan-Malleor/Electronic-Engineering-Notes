Potential energy across two points

Analogy:

1. The battery pushes electrons to the "top" of the mountain
2. When the electron is at the "top" of the mountain and begins to "roll" downhill there is a potential difference between the "top" of the hill and the "bottom", where the electron heads back to the power source, like a battery, to be "pushed"  back up the mountain
	1. As the electron is "rolling downhill" we can put components along the way so the charges can do some work
