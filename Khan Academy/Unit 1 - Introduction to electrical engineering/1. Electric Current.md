The idea of charge:
* We have two type of charges: - (Cation) , + (Anion)
	* Like a magnet opposite charges are going to attract and like charges are going to repel

We have to get charge and one of the places to get charge is copper wires
* In copper it has 1 electron is the outermost orbit, which makes it easier to pull that electron away to be used in current
* In many atoms, the outermost electrons (valence electrons) are held very weakly by the positively charged nucleus. When multiple atoms bond together (like in a solid piece of metal), these outer electrons "detach" from their parent atoms and can roam anywhere across the solid.
