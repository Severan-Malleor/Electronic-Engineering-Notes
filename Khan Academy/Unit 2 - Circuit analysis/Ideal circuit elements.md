Three circuit elements:
1. Resistor - R
2. Capacitor - C
3. Inductors - L

# Equations

Resistance (Ohms law):
#### $V = I \times R$ 

Capacitor:
The current flowing into or out of a capacitor is equal to its capacitance (C in Farads) multiplied by how fast the voltage across it is changing (dV/dt).
### $I = C \,\text{(Capacitance)} \times \frac{dV}{dt}$

Inductor:
The voltage across an inductor is equal to its inductance (L in Henrys) multiplied by how fast the current through it is changing (dI/dt).
### $V = L \,\text{(Inductance)} \times \frac{dI}{dt}$
